## Netherlands Legal Context

- **OM Aanwijzing kinderpornografie (2024)** — The Dutch prosecution guidelines on CSAM. Since 1 July 2024, CSAM falls under article 252 of the Dutch Criminal Code (formerly 240b). Maximum 6 years; up to 9 years when habitual or professional (article 254).
  <https://www.om.nl/onderwerpen/beleidsregels/aanwijzingen/jeugd---gezin---zeden/aanwijzing-kinderpornografie-2024a006>

## Help Services

- **Preventielijn van Offlimits (Netherlands)** — Anonymous, free phone and chat. 0800 266 64 36. Formerly Stop it Now Nederland. Also offers peer groups and conversation guides for partners and family.
  <https://preventielijn.offlimits.nl>

- **Stoppen is mogelijk (Netherlands / Flanders)** — Anonymous self-help module for people worried about their viewing behaviour.
  <https://stoppen-is-mogelijk.eu>

- **Stop It Now UK and Ireland (Lucy Faithfull Foundation)** — Helpline: 0808 1000 900.
  <https://www.stopitnow.org.uk>

- **ReDirection (Protect Children, Finland)** — Multilingual anonymous self-help programme.
  <https://www.suojellaanlapsia.fi/en/redirection>

## Warning Message Evidence

- **reThink Chatbot Evaluation on Pornhub UK (2022-2023)** — University of Tasmania. Shown 2.8M times; 82% no repeat search; 490 click-throughs; ~68 calls to Stop It Now.
  <https://figshare.utas.edu.au/articles/report/reThink_Chatbot_Evaluation/25320859>

- **Project Intercept — Lucy Faithfull Foundation (May 2026)** — 70M+ warnings across Google, TikTok, Meta, ChatGPT, Mega and adult sites; ~683K click-throughs (~1 in 100).
  <https://nominet.uk/news/more-than-70-million-attempts-to-access-child-sexual-abuse-imagery-intercepted-via-warning-messages-charity-reveals/>

- **Redesigned Google Search Warning (preprint, June 2026)** — New messaging cut follow-up searches by 3.8 percentage points.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06126>

- **Prichard et al. (2024) — Randomised warning trial, honeypot site** — 3/4 of control entered vs 35-47% after warning. Deterrent and therapeutic messages both worked; adding images did not help.
  <https://researchcommons.waikato.ac.nz/items/e5453eac-e5e2-4a0a-b60d-056f24f799bd>

- **Ahmia.fi dark web warning messages (2025-2026)** — 3M+ warned; CTR raised from 8.7% to 15.7%; harm-focused message worked best.
  - Protect Children, Preventive Pathways report: <https://www.suojellaanlapsia.fi/en/post/preventive-pathways-csam-warnings-research-report>
  - Ciardha, Caoilte O., Joel Scanlan, Tegan Insoll, Juha Nurmi, and Nina Vaaranen-Valkonen. "Warning Message Content Increases Help Seeking in a Large-Scale Dark Web CSAM Intervention." arXiv preprint arXiv:2606.06417 (2026). <https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.06417>

## Treatment and Self-Help

- **Prevent It (Karolinska Institutet)** — Randomised placebo-controlled trial of anonymous online CBT. Small but significant reduction in CSAM use.
  <https://news.ki.se/reduced-use-of-child-sexual-abuse-material-after-online-cbt>

- **ReDirection survey (Protect Children, Finland)** — ~Half of 17K+ respondents want to stop; only 3% had received help.
  <https://www.suojellaanlapsia.fi/en/redirection>
