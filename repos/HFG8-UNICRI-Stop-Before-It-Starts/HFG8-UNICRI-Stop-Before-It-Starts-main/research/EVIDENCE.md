# Evidence Summaries

This document summarises the key evidence on warning messages and prevention interventions for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Each entry includes the key findings and the source link. Items marked [VERIFY] need independent confirmation before citing in a submission.

---

## 1. reThink Chatbot on Pornhub UK (2022-2023)

A warning page plus reThink chatbot deployed on Pornhub UK.

**Key findings:**
- Shown 2.8 million times
- 82% of sessions saw no repeat abuse-related search after the warning
- 490 click-throughs to help resources
- Approximately 68 calls or chats to Stop It Now

**Source:** University of Tasmania, reThink Chatbot Evaluation
<https://figshare.utas.edu.au/articles/report/reThink_Chatbot_Evaluation/25320859>

---

## 2. Project Intercept (Lucy Faithfull Foundation)

Warning messages deployed across Google, TikTok, Meta, ChatGPT, Mega and adult sites, covering 22 interventions over two years (reported May 2026).

**Key findings:**
- More than 70 million warnings shown
- Approximately 683,000 click-throughs to help (~1 in 100)

**Source:** Nominet / Lucy Faithfull Foundation
<https://nominet.uk/news/more-than-70-million-attempts-to-access-child-sexual-abuse-imagery-intercepted-via-warning-messages-charity-reveals/>

---

## 3. Redesigned Google Search Warning (2026)

A redesigned warning message on Google Search, tested against the previous message format.

**Key findings:**
- New messaging reduced follow-up abuse-related searches in the same session by 3.8 percentage points versus the old message

**Source:** Google Search preprint (June 2026)
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06126>

---

## 4. Randomised Warning Trial — Honeypot Site (Prichard et al. 2024)

A randomised controlled trial using a honeypot site, targeting men aged 18 to 30.

**Key findings:**
- 3 out of 4 (75%) of the control group entered the site
- Only 35-47% entered after seeing a warning
- Both deterrent and therapeutic messages were effective
- Adding images to the warning did not improve effectiveness

**Source:** Prichard et al., Child Abuse & Neglect (2024)
<https://researchcommons.waikato.ac.nz/items/e5453eac-e5e2-4a0a-b60d-056f24f799bd>

---

## 5. Warning Messages on Ahmia.fi Dark Web Search Engine (2025-2026)

Redesigned warning messages tested on the Ahmia.fi dark web search engine.

**Key findings:**
- Over 3 million searches triggered a warning
- Redesigned messages raised click-through to help from 8.7% to 15.7%
- A harm-focused message (emphasising harm to children) produced the most clicks to help

**Sources:**
- Protect Children, Preventive Pathways report: <https://www.suojellaanlapsia.fi/en/post/preventive-pathways-csam-warnings-research-report>
- Ciardha et al. (2026): <https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.06417>

---

## 6. ReDirection Programme Survey (Finland)

An anonymous multilingual self-help programme run by Protect Children (Finland), with a large-scale survey of users.

**Key findings:**
- Approximately half of more than 17,000 survey respondents say they want to stop
- Only 3% report having received any help

**Source:** Protect Children, ReDirection programme
<https://www.suojellaanlapsia.fi/en/redirection>

---

## 7. Prevent It Online Therapy (Karolinska Institutet)

Randomised controlled trials of anonymous online CBT-based therapy for people who use CSAM.

**Key findings:**
- Three randomised, controlled trials have found significant positive effects both on actual offending behaviour towards children as well as on risk factors for future abuse

**Source:** Karolinska Institutet news release
<https://news.ki.se/reduced-use-of-child-sexual-abuse-material-after-online-cbt>

## Key Takeaways for Design

| Finding | Design implication |
|---------|-------------------|
| 82% did not search again after seeing a warning (reThink) | Warnings work — even simple ones interrupt behaviour |
| Only ~1 in 100 click through to help (Project Intercept) | The jump from warning to help is the biggest drop-off point |
| Harm-focused messages got the most help clicks (Ahmia.fi) | Lead with child harm, not just legal consequences |
| Deterrent messages best at stopping entry (Prichard et al.) | Legal consequences are effective for prevention |
| Half want to stop, only 3% got help (ReDirection) | Demand for help far exceeds supply and access |
| 3.8pp reduction with redesigned message (Google) | Message design matters — iterate and test variants |

## Netherlands Legal Context

Since 1 July 2024, CSAM falls under **article 252** of the Dutch Criminal Code (formerly article 240b).

- Maximum sentence: **6 years**
- Up to **9 years** when done habitually or professionally (article 254)
- Viewing material online is covered

**Source:** OM Aanwijzing kinderpornografie
<https://www.om.nl/onderwerpen/beleidsregels/aanwijzingen/jeugd---gezin---zeden/aanwijzing-kinderpornografie-2024a006>
