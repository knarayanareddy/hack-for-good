# Personas

These personas are composites based on the stages used in prevention research and the challenge brief. They are not real individuals.

---

## The Platform Persona

**Profile:** A Dutch hobby forum with 20,000 members and one part-time moderator.

**Who they are:** The moderator (or small trust-and-safety team) at a small or mid-sized platform in the Netherlands. They may be a trust and safety lead at a forum, a developer at a gaming chat, the operator of an adult site, someone running a hosting or file-sharing service, or a moderator team at a social or dating app. They know they should do something about harmful search behaviour on their platform, but they have little time and budget, cannot build their own detection or warning system, and worry about privacy law and their users' trust.

**What might move them:** A solution that is free, open-source, requires minimal technical effort to install (days, not months), does not require them to handle sensitive data, comes with clear documentation, and gives them confidence they are doing the right thing legally and ethically.

**Where they can be reached:** Tech conferences, platform operator communities, trust and safety forums, hosting provider networks, and through UNICRI's own outreach.

## 01 — Tempted (has not acted)

**Who they are:** Has sexual thoughts about children but has never searched for or viewed abuse material. Feels drawn to it, worries about it, often carries shame and has nobody to talk to.

**What might move them:** Knowing they are not alone. Knowing that the feelings are not their fault. Knowing that help is anonymous. Understanding that acting on these thoughts is a choice, and that choosing to seek help is possible before any line is crossed.

**Where they can be reached:** Search engines, adult sites, forums, gaming and social platforms, health information sites, and general awareness campaigns.

---

## 02 — Starting (first searches)

**Who they are:** Has started searching for or viewing abuse material, or has had a first sexualised chat with a minor. May tell themselves it is harmless or that "just looking" does not count.

**What might move them:** A clear message that this is a crime, that children are harmed by the creation and distribution of this material, and that they can still stop — with one concrete next step they can take right now.

**Where they can be reached:** Search results, adult platforms, gaming platforms, chat and social apps — the places where the first searches happen.

---

## 03 — In Deep (repeated viewing, sharing or contact)

**Who they are:** Views or shares material repeatedly, or has had contact with children. Many say they want to stop, but most never receive help. Research shows that approximately half of more than 17,000 survey respondents in the ReDirection programme said they wanted to stop, but only 3% had received any help.

**What might move them:** Consequences that are real and immediate. Help that exists even now, even after repeated behaviour. Evidence that treatment works (the Prevent It trial showed a small but significant reduction in use of abuse material after online CBT).

**Where they can be reached:** The platforms where the behaviour happens, and after detection or arrest.

---

## 04 — People Around Them (partners, family, friends, professionals)

**Who they are:** Worried about someone close to them — a partner, family member, friend — or about a client or patient. Unsure how to raise it, unsure what to do, unsure where to refer.

**What might move them:** Guidance on how to start the conversation. Knowledge of where to refer. Reassurance that seeking advice is the right thing to do.

**Where they can be reached:** The Offlimits prevention line (0800 266 64 36), GPs, schools, employers, and professional networks.

---

## Design Implications

| Persona | Primary message | Primary channel | Key barrier |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Platform | "Switch it on in a day" | Developer docs, integration guides | Time, budget, legal worry |
| 01 Tempted | "You are not alone, help is anonymous" | Search engines, health sites | Shame, isolation |
| 02 Starting | "This is a crime. Children are harmed. You can still stop." | Search results, platform warnings | Denial, minimisation |
| 03 In deep | "Help exists even now. Treatment works." | Platform warnings, post-detection | Hopelessness, fear of exposure |
| 04 People around them | "Here is how to start the conversation" | Prevention lines, GPs | Not knowing what to say |
