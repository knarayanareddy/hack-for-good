## Safe Test Plan

_How could UNICRI, a platform or a research partner measure whether your solution works — without exposing anyone to harm?_

_Address each of these:_

1. **What you would measure:** _e.g., warning-shown count, click-through rate to help, session abandonment rate_
2. **How you avoid tracking individuals:** _e.g., aggregate counters only, no user IDs, counts suppressed below 10_
3. **How you avoid using real abuse-related terms:** _e.g., synthetic trigger lists with `test-trigger-*` prefix only_
