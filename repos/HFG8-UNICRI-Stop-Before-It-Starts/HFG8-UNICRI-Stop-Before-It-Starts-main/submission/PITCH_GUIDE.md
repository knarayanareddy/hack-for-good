# Pitch Guide

Your pitch is **3 minutes** followed by **2 minutes of Q&A** from the jury.

---

## Structure

### Minute 1 — The Problem and Your Target User

- **Open with the gap.** Warnings and help exist, but they reach too few people on too few platforms. Most platforms show no warning at all, and even where warnings run, only about 1 in 100 people click through to help.
- **Name your target user.** Which persona stage are you designing for? (01 Tempted, 02 Starting, 03 In deep, 04 People around them.) Which platform type?
- **Make it concrete.** Use the example from the brief: a Dutch hobby forum with 20,000 members and one part-time moderator who wants a warning and a route to help live within a week.

### Minute 2 — Live Demo

- **Show, do not tell.** Run your prototype on the mock platform. Type `test-trigger-red` to trigger the warning.
- **Walk through the user journey:** trigger, warning shown, route to help, what happens next.
- **Show the platform side:** how the operator installs it, how they see aggregate impact.
- **Highlight your creative angle.** What is new about your format or approach?

### Minute 3 — Evidence, Impact Measurement, Next Steps

- **Ground it in evidence.** Which research findings support your design? (See [research/EVIDENCE.md](../research/EVIDENCE.md).)
- **Explain your safe test plan.** How would you measure impact without tracking individuals or exposing anyone to harm?
- **Next steps.** What would you build with three more months? Which platforms and research partners would you work with?
- **End with one sentence** on why this matters.

---

## Jury Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What the jury looks for |
|-----------|--------|------------------------|
| **Impact on prevention** | 25% | Plausibly stops behaviour or moves people to help, grounded in evidence |
| **Adoptability** | 25% | Easy to switch on, low cost, works across languages, accessible |
| **Creativity** | 20% | A new angle on format or packaging, not a copy of an existing pop-up |
| **Safety and ethics** | 20% | Protects children first, respects privacy, honest about confidentiality, no harm from testing |
| **Clarity of pitch** | 10% | Clear problem, user, solution and next step |

---

## Tips

- **Stay within 3 minutes.** Practice with a timer. The jury will stop you.
- **Do not read from slides.** Talk to the jury, show the prototype.
- **Anticipate questions.** Common ones:
  - "How does this work in a country with different confidentiality rules?"
  - "What stops someone from dismissing the warning?"
  - "How do you avoid habituation on repeated views?"
  - "What data does the platform need to share?"
  - "How do you know it works if you cannot track individuals?"
- **Be honest about limitations.** The jury values honesty over polish.
- **Remember the safety rules.** Never use real abuse-related search terms in your demo. Only `test-trigger-*` terms.
