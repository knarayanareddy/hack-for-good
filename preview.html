The problem

In a crisis, the people who know the ground truth are hard to find, hard to weigh, and often cannot speak freely.

Where data is thin and access is limited, expert opinion is often the best signal there is. But it arrives unscored and unweighted, from people who may be at risk for what they say. Analysts cannot tell whose judgment holds up.

How we solve it

Ninety seconds from each expert, scored when reality answers.

An invitation-only circle of humanitarian experts forecasts short, resolvable questions drawn from ACAPS's own risks.

1

Ask

A risk becomes a precise question with a window and a public source that will settle it, sent to experts who know that country or theme.

2

Estimate, blind

Each expert gives a probability before seeing ACAPS's reading or anyone else's.

3

See the picture

The circle's pooled range, ACAPS's reading and your own mark, side by side. You may revise.

4

Resolve and score

When the source settles it, each forecast can be scored against the outcome, so reliability is measured, not assumed.
