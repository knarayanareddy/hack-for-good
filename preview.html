The problem · Slide 1 of 2

Humanitarian risk ratings are judgments. We rarely learn how well they hold up.

Analysts rate each risk from Very Low to Very High. Years later we know what happened, but there is no routine way to score the ratings, learn from the experts who saw it coming, or tell good judgment from lucky guesses.

How we solve it · Slide 2 of 2

Ninety seconds from each expert, scored when reality answers.

An invitation-only circle of humanitarian experts forecasts short, resolvable questions drawn from ACAPS's own risks.

1

Ask

A risk becomes a precise question with a window and a public source that will settle it.

2

Estimate, blind

Each expert gives a probability before seeing ACAPS's reading or anyone else's.

3

See the picture

The circle's pooled range, ACAPS's reading and your own mark, side by side. You may revise.

4

Resolve and score

When the source settles it, every forecast is scored, so the circle learns who is well calibrated.
