SLIDE 1 · THE BOTTLENECK

The Problem: Crisis Intelligence is Fragile & Slow

ACAPS monitors 130+ crises using bilateral phone calls and qualitative check-ins. This model breaks down under pressure:

⏳ 45-Minute Unstructured Asks

Frontline humanitarians spend 45–60 mins on subjective bilateral interviews. Survey fatigue causes massive drop-off, leaving critical regional gaps.

⚓ Cognitive Anchoring & Groupthink

When experts see an ACAPS rating or dominant peer opinion first, they anchor to the herd. Crucial minority ground signals get silenced.

🛡️ Severe Frontline Protection Risks

Humanitarians in sensitive conflict zones (Chad, Sudan, Syria) cannot safely put their real name on political forecasts without severe physical risk.

🕳️ The "Black Hole" Retention Crisis

Experts share their knowledge but never hear how their insight shifted the analysis or if their judgment was accurate, killing motivation.

The Reality: 45m calls · High fatigue · Anchored groupthink · Zero quantitative feedback UNSUSTAINABLE →
SLIDE 2 · THE SOLUTION

The Solution: Ground Truth Circle

"Turning 45-minute field interviews into 90-second structured forecasts — without anchoring, groupthink, or fear."

⚡ 90-Second Delphi Forecast Card

Drag a probability slider (5–95%) + confidence interval (±4% to ±15%). Asynchronous, mobile-ready, respect responder time. 45 mins reduced to 90 seconds.

🔓 "Give-to-Get" Anti-Anchoring Gate

Neither ACAPS ratings nor peer consensus are visible until the responder commits their independent estimate. Eliminates herd bias completely.

🔒 4-Tier Anonymity & Retroactive Retreat

Named, Alias, Sealed (ACAPS only), Anonymous. If local conflict escalates, responders downgrade past public footprints with 1 click.

💡 Structured Roundtable & Impact Receipts

Debate via structured claims & epistemic reactions ("Changed my mind"). When dissent shifts an analyst, an automated receipt closes the loop.
