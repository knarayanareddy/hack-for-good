Hackathon for Good #8 | @ The Hague Tech 🚀

48 hours. Real challenges. Real impact.

The Hague's most purpose-driven hackathon is back for its 8th edition. From Friday 6 to Sunday 8 November 2026, innovators, developers, designers, and changemakers gather at The Hague Tech for 48 hours of hacking, mentoring, pitching, and building solutions that actually matter.

This is not your average hackathon. The challenges are real. The mentors are experts. The energy is electric.

💡 Who can join?

Whether you're a developer, data scientist, designer, product thinker, or a domain expert with a passion for impact — you belong here.

  • Teams of 2 to 6 people
  • No team yet? No problem, after registering you can find teammates or create your own team directly on the platform
  • All skill levels welcome, from seasoned hackers to first-timers
  • Challenges come from governments, NGOs, and purpose-driven organisations

The only requirement: a hacker mindset and the drive to build something meaningful.

🔥 What can you expect?

Over 48 hours, you'll move through a full innovation sprint, from challenge briefing to working prototype to live pitch.

  • 🧠 Real challenges from organisations working on societal impact
  • 👥 Expert mentors available throughout the weekend to guide your team
  • 🛠️ Access to data, tools, and resources to build your solution
  • 🎤 Pitch to a jury of professionals on Sunday afternoon
  • 🏆 Prizes for the best solutions, including an overall winner

ℹ️ Practical Info

  • 📅 Date: 6–8 November 2026
  • 📍 Location: The Hague Tech, Waldorpstraat 5, 2521 CA Den Haag
  • ⏱️ Duration: 48 hours
  • 👥 Team size: 2–6 people
  • 🌙 Overnight: You get a discount code for The Social Hub
  • 🍽️ Catering: Meals and snacks provided throughout the weekend
  • 💰 Cost: Free to participate
  • ✉️ Questions: info@hackathonforgood.org

👉 Ready to hack for good?

Register now, then find or create your team on the platform. Spots are limited, don't wait!

Have a question? Reach out at info@hackathonforgood.org, we'd love to hear from you.
