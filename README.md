## 🎯 Challenges Overview

### 1. ACAPS Ground Truth
* **Challenge**: How can ACAPS turn the scattered knowledge of humanitarian experts into sharper, better grounded risk analysis, without costing those experts their time or their anonymity?
* **Path**: `repos/HFG8-ACAPS-Ground-Truth/HFG8-ACAPS-Ground-Truth-main`

### 2. UNICRI — Stop Before It Starts
* **Challenge**: Safe, ethical early intervention tooling and assistance to protect vulnerable individuals online before harm occurs.
* **Path**: `repos/HFG8-UNICRI-Stop-Before-It-Starts/HFG8-UNICRI-Stop-Before-It-Starts-main`
